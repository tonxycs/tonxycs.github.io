About

I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie PhD student at the University of Cambridge under the supervision of Prof. Cecilia Mascolo. My research focuses on vocal biomarkers for uncertainty-aware grounded conversational AI for health assessment.

Driven by curiosity and a commitment to innovation, I aim to harness AI to address practical challenges and improve quality of life though digital healthcare.

My path, from a BSc and MSc in Computer Engineering (AI/ML) at the University of Calabria to international research and industry experiences in Luxembourg and the United Kingdom, has shaped a multidisciplinary and collaborative approach to scientific inquiry.

Beyond research, I am passionate about tennis and write about science for Varsity Science.
