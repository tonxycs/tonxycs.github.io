About

Hi! I am Xinyu Tong 💇, a first-year M.S. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), advised by Prof. Lei Wang.

My research focuses on developing efficient CV/ML algorithms for multimodal AI across multidomain imaging applications, including medical imaging, remote‑sensing imagery and other multimodal data. Specifically, I work on integrating computer vision techniques to build lightweight, robust, general, secure foundational models and fusing them with Multimodal large language models/Agents.

I have focused on these topics recently 📖:
• Multimodal (Medical AI, LLM)        • AI4S (Health, Earth, Cell)

Beyond research, I am passionate about basketball, running and music 🎵🏀🏃
